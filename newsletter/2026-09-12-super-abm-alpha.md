## Trade Offer Accepted! New Name!
Shoutouts to TopNep from the ABM Discord for blessing the project! But, uh, I’m renaming and reconceptualizing the just-announced Super Rock Paper Scissors Online project. It’s a shame, because the logo was sweet.

[rip logo]

It had a branding problem and a design problem:
1. Bad name. I wanted just 'Super RPS' but there’s an existing game on Steam with that name. Super Rock Paper Scissors Online was an awkward attempt at differentiation. 
2. No progression. Players had no reason to keep playing and coming back.

   So I’m changing the name to Super ABM and building a progression system next. Maybe the variant gauntlet idea can come back later.
