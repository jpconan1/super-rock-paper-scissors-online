
**Lucky** 🍀 
• If you are attacked when you use mana, you have a **1 in 4** chance of surviving and getting the mana anyway.

**Advantaged** 🔪 
• Using mana on any of the first **three turns** gives you **2 mana** instead of 1.

**Thief** 💰 
• **Steal:** Before making your moves, you may choose to steal 1 mana from your opponent. This mana will be stolen **after the moves have been resolved**.
• It may **not** be on the **first four turns** of the game.
• You may use the ability **once per game**.
• If your opponent has **zero mana** following moves being resolved, you will gain nothing and your ability will have been wasted.
• If **both players** attempt to steal on the same turn, the abilities are both wasted.

**Juggernaut** 💪 
• If you attack **two turns in a row**, your opponent cannot block on the **next turn**.
*Example: Player one attacks on Turns 1 & 2, player two cannot block on Turn 3.*
• This effect only activates on even numbers of consecutive attacks (i.e. it will only activate on 2 attacks in a row, 4 attacks in a row, etc.)

**Stunner** 💥 
• When you attack, your opponent's attack cost **next turn** will **double**.
• When you use something other than an attack, their attack cost will **reset to one**.
*Example: Player one attacks on Turn 1, Player two's attack cost on Turn 2 will be 2 mana. If Player one attack's on Turn 2, player two's attack cost on Turn 3 will be 4 mana. If they use a block or mana, it would reset to 1 mana.*

**Duplicator** 🦠 
• When you mana, the mana you will gain **next turn** if you use mana will **double**.
• When you use something other than mana, the mana gained will reset to **one mana**.
*Example: Player one uses mana, they gain 1 mana. If they use mana on the next turn, and gain 2 mana. If they use anything else, then the mana gained on the next turn resets to 1.*

**Gambler** 🎰 
• On blocking, one of eight random effects happens, with the following chances:
  **30% chance**: Nothing happens.
  **10% chance**: Lose 1 block.
  **15% chance**: Gain 1 block.
  **5% chance**: Gain 2 blocks.
  **10% chance**: Lose all your mana.
  **10% chance**: Double your mana.
  **19% chance**: Gain one mana.
  **1% chance**: Gain 2 mana.
• You can only block **3 times in a row**, as opposed to 5. Using anything other than a block will reset blocks to 3.

**Tax Collector** 🧾 
• **Collect:** Before making your moves, you can choose to deduct 1 mana from both players. This will occur **after the moves have been resolved**.
• You must have **at least 1 mana** to use this ability.
• You may only use it **three times per game**.
• If a player only has **0 mana** when **mana is deducted**, they will **not lose any mana**.

**Copywriter** ✍️ 
• When your opponent does any move **three times in a row**, you gain **1 mana**.
• This mana is added **before moves are resolved**.

**Conjurer** 🪄 
• **Conjure:** Before making your moves, you may choose to make this round **conjured**.
• On a conjured round, your **opponent** will make their move before you, and you will be told what their move was.
• Using this ability costs **1 mana**.
• You may use this ability **two times per game**.
If **both players** select conjurer and they **both** decide to **conjure** nothing will happen.

**The Sumo** 🤼 
• If both you and your opponent **both attack**, you do **not lose a mana**.
• This effect may only happen **three times per game**.

**Fireborne** 🔥 
• **Flame:** Before making your moves, you can choose to gain an extra life. This will occur **after the moves have been resolved**.
• The extra life lasts for **5 turns**.
• If you would have lost the game while you have the extra life, your **extra life is consumed** and you remain in the game.
• After **5 turns**, the extra life expires.
• Using the ability costs **1 mana**.
• You may use this ability **once per game**.

**Retired** 👴 
• Start with **7 mana**.
• You cannot gain mana **for any reason**. You may still use the mana move, but it will not provide any mana.
• If both players are **retired**, and you both drop to **0 mana**, the game will **reset without classes**.
• You can only block **4 times in a row**, as opposed to 5. Using anything other than a block will reset blocks to 4.

**Parrymaster** 🌟 
• **Parry:** Before making your moves, you may choose to attempt to **parry** this turn.
• If you parry, and your opponent **attacks this turn**, they **lose 2 mana**.
• If they do not attack, nothing happens, and the ability is **wasted**.
• You may use this ability **once per game**.


**Cheater** 🤫 
• When you mana, you have a **1 in 3 chance** of gaining **2 mana**, instead of 1.

**Cupid** 💘 
• **Golden Arrow:** Before making your moves, you can choose to use this ability. This will occur **after the moves have been resolved**.
• The ability will last for **5 turns**.
• If during the ability, both players use the **same move** (excluding skip), the following effects will occur:
  **Both players attack:** You will **not lose 1 mana** for your attack.
  **Both players block:** Your opponent **loses 1 extra block**.
  **Both players mana:** You gain **1 extra mana**.
• You may use this move **once per game**.

**Investor** 📉 
• Start with **5 mana**.
• Every third turn, lose **1 mana**.
• If both opponents use mana on the same turn, you will gain 2 mana instead of 1

**Defender** 🛡️ 
• When you **successfully block an attack**, it will **not cause you to lose one of your consecutive blocks remaining**.

**Last Ditch** 🙏 
• If both **you and your opponent** drop to **0 mana**, you will gain **2 mana** instead of 1, but every 2nd reset that happens will have your mana gain increased by 1. 2-3-3-4-4-5-5 etc.

**Null** ⚫ 
• **Reset:** Before making your moves, you may choose to **reset the game**.
• This occurs **before all abilities and moves**.
• This will reset the game to **an initial state**.
• This will **not reset strikes, nor will it reset the turn count**.
• You may use this ability **once per game**.

**Joe** 👨 
**THIS ROLE IS NOT LEGAL IN LEAGUE/TOURNAMENT PLAY UNLESS OTHERWISE STATED**
• **Every turn**, you have a **1 in a million chance** to gain **infinite mana**.
• Otherwise, does **nothing**.
• Good luck.
