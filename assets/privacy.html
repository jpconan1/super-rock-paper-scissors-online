Super ABM Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Super ABM is a non-commercial community project. We will not sell, rent, trade, or give away your personal information. We will not use personal information for advertising or sell advertising space in the game.

We may use a news or community section to promote Super ABM or other projects, including commercial projects made by us, our friends, or our partners.

Information we collect

You can play as a guest. When you do, we collect or create:

  • your chosen display name;
  • a random player identifier and an authentication secret;
  • your rating and game records, including opponents, results, and gameplay summaries;
  • technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as your IP address, browser or device information, request times, and connection activity; and
  • content you choose to submit through multiplayer features, such as the shared whiteboard.

Your guest identifier, authentication secret, and display name are also stored in your browser so the game can recognize you when you return. Sound and interface preferences may be stored in your browser. You can remove this browser data through your browser settings, but doing so may prevent you from recovering the same guest profile.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we also receive information from Google needed to create and secure your account. This may include your name, email address, profile image, Google account identifier, and authentication tokens. Super ABM does not receive your Google password.

We do not intentionally collect payment information, precise location, contacts, private Google content, or information for targeted advertising.

How we use information

We use information only to:

  • provide guest and signed-in accounts;
  • operate matchmaking, multiplayer games, ratings, leaderboards, and community features;
  • save game results and maintain the integrity of rankings;
  • secure accounts, limit abuse, investigate technical problems, and keep the service reliable;
  • respond to privacy, support, or safety requests; and
  • comply with applicable law.

We do not use personal information to train artificial intelligence models.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information or give it to others for their own marketing or advertising.

We use service providers to run the game. Cloudflare hosts the game, network services, and database. Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies, and information may be processed outside your province or outside Canada, where it may be subject to local law.

Your display name, rating, online presence, match participation, and content you submit to shared multiplayer features may be visible to other players or the public as part of the game.

We may disclose personal information when Canadian law requires or permits us to do so, including in response to a valid request, court order, warrant, or other lawful process from Canadian law enforcement or another Canadian public authority. We may challenge or reject requests that we believe are invalid or too broad. We will not voluntarily provide personal information to law enforcement where we are not legally permitted to do so.

Retention

Account and player records are kept while they are needed to operate Super ABM. Match results and rating records may be kept for the life of the game because they support rankings, game history, and fair play. Security and connection records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Temporary matchmaking, live-game, lobby, and whiteboard data may be deleted or overwritten as those sessions expire.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or remove identifying details, subject to technical limitations, backups, record integrity, and legal requirements.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a small community project. These include encrypted connections, access controls, hashed guest secrets on the server, and encryption of Google authentication tokens. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

Keep your device and account secure. Anyone with access to the guest authentication secret stored in your browser may be able to use that guest profile.

Your choices and rights

Google sign-in is optional. You may play as a guest instead.

You may ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold personal information about you;
  • provide access to that information;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • withdraw consent where applicable; or
  • delete information associated with your account or guest profile.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security, or to preserve the integrity of match and rating records. We may ask for information needed to verify that the account or guest profile belongs to you before fulfilling a request.

You can disconnect Super ABM from your Google Account through Google's account controls. This stops future Google access but does not automatically delete information already held by Super ABM. Contact us to request deletion from Super ABM.

Children

Super ABM is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or our privacy practices change. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date. If a change materially expands how we collect, use, or disclose personal information, we will provide a prominent notice and seek consent where required.
