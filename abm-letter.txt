# Dear ABM Community,

### I made your Discord game into a web client!

I already had a working client and most of the art done through another game I’m building, Super Rock Paper Scissors Online, which I adapted for this project.

(s-rps-o logo)

Super Rock Paper Scissors Online is planned to be a competitive arena for Rock Paper Scissors variants. Each season will feature nine variants, and players pick/ban them in best-of-three matches for Elo. After a few months, I’ll rotate/patch/invent new variants, award the top-ranked players some cosmetic prize, and reset the rankings for the next season!

You can play a prototype right now at [https://rps.jpconan.ca/](https://rps.jpconan.ca/)
